# ZMIE TRADITIONAL KNOWLEDGE & EPISTEMIC PRESERVATION MODEL
## Unani, Ayurveda, Siddha & TCM Ontology Without Reductive Flattening

---

### 1. The Core Philosophical Mandate

> **"Unify the world's medical knowledge without pretending that every medical system says the same thing."**

ZMIE strictly prohibits reducing classical paradigms into modern biochemical equivalents. For example:
- **Unani *Balgham*** is NOT simply "respiratory phlegm / mucus". It is a physiological humour governing coolness and moisture throughout bodily tissues.
- **Ayurvedic *Vata*** is NOT simply "nerve impulses". It is the foundational principle of biological kinetic energy and catabolism.
- **TCM *Pi* (Spleen)** is NOT the anatomical spleen. It is the functional system of nutrient extraction, distribution, and blood containment.

### 3. Codex Monograph Schema

Canonical texts are ingested with structured metadata fields:
1. **`codex_name`**: Formal historical title (e.g. *Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb*).
2. **`author`**: Primary author or lineage (e.g. Ibn Sina).
3. **`volume_and_chapter`**: Structural book and maqala reference.
4. **`original_language`**: Arabic, Persian, Sanskrit, Classical Chinese.
5. **`temperament_mizaj`**: Damavi (Sanguine), Safrawi (Choleric), Balghami (Phlegmatic), Sawdawi (Melancholic); Warmth/Moisture degrees (1st to 4th degree).
6. **`ayurvedic_rasa_panchaka`**: Rasa (taste), Guna (attribute), Virya (potency), Vipaka (post-digestive effect), Prabhava (unique action).
7. **`safety_and_muslih`**: Correctives (*Muslih* in Unani, *Shodhana* purification in Ayurveda) required to mitigate toxicity.
