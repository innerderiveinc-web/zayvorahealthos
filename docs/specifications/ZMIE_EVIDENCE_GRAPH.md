# ZMIE EVIDENCE GRAPH SPECIFICATION
## Empirical Evidence Grounding, Evidence Ladder & Contradiction Architecture

---

### 1. The Evidence Ladder ($E_0$ through $E_6$)

ZMIE rejects simple count-based evidence metrics (e.g. *"10 studies = strong evidence"*). All claims are ranked along a controlled, non-promotable Evidence Ladder:

| Level | Designation | Description | Clinical Actionability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$E_6$** | Controlled Clinical Evidence | Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled human clinical trials (RCTs). | Primary standard for clinical efficacy assertions. |
| **$E_5$** | Human Observational / Exposure | Prospective cohort, retrospective case-control, registry studies, human PK/PD. | Validates associations, real-world tolerability, and epidemiology. |
| **$E_4$** | Preclinical *In-Vivo* Models | Rodent, canine, non-human primate animal models of disease. | Demonstrates biological activity; strictly prohibited from claiming human efficacy. |
| **$E_3$** | Mechanistic *In-Vitro* | Immortalized cell lines, primary human cultures, organoids, binding assays. | Elucidates molecular receptor mechanisms and enzymatic kinetics. |
| **$E_2$** | Computational *In-Silico* | Molecular docking, quantitative structure-activity relationship (QSAR), simulations. | Generates mechanistic hypotheses; zero clinical verification. |
| **$E_1$** | Literature Association | Retrospective review, textbook mentions, co-occurrence in bibliographic indices. | Contextual background only. |
| **$E_0$** | AI-Generated Hypothesis | Computational reasoning paths generated by autonomous research agents. | Flagged as unverified speculative paths. |

---

### 2. Evidence Status

Distinct from the methodological Evidence Level, every evidence node carries an **Evidence Status**:
- `SUPPORTS`
- `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTS`
- `MIXED`
- `CONTRADICTS`
- `INSUFFICIENT`
- `NO_EFFECT`
- `UNKNOWN`

---

### 3. The Contradiction Model (Section 20)

In medical science, conflicting findings are normal and scientifically informative. ZMIE **preserves both supporting and contradicting studies** attached to the same relationship edge.

The system decomposes disagreement into ten objective axes:
1. Different Population (e.g. Asian cohort vs Western cohort)
2. Different Dose (e.g. 1500mg/day vs 300mg/day)
3. Different Formulation (e.g. 97% pure berberine HCl vs crude root decoction)
4. Different Endpoint (e.g. HbA1c at 16 weeks vs fasting blood glucose at 2 weeks)
5. Different Duration (e.g. acute postprandial vs 6-month chronic therapy)
6. Different Methodology & Risk of Bias
7. Different Species (human vs rodent model)
8. Different Disease Severity or Staging
9. Different Study Statistical Power ($n=1200$ vs $n=24$)
10. Historical Period & Standard-of-Care Co-medications
