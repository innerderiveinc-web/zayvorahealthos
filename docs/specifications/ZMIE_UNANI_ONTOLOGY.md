### 2. Core Entities

#### A. Mizaj (Temperament)
1. **Damwi (Sanguine)**: Hot & Moist (*Harr Ratab*). High vascularity, metabolic warmth.
2. **Safrawi (Choleric)**: Hot & Dry (*Harr Yabis*). Sharp metabolic drive, bilious tendencies.
3. **Balghami (Phlegmatic)**: Cold & Moist (*Barid Ratab*). Sluggish metabolic fire, moisture retention.
4. **Sawdawi (Melancholic)**: Cold & Dry (*Barid Yabis*). High visceral tension, dry tissue tone.

#### B. Quwwat-e-Hazima (Digestive Faculty)
Decomposed into four sequential systemic actions across organs:
- **Hazm-e-Medi**: Gastric digestion in the stomach (*Meda*).
- **Hazm-e-Kabidi**: Hepatic transformation in the liver (*Jigar*), producing the four humors.
- **Hazm-e-Uruqi**: Vascular digestion in systemic circulation.
- **Hazm-e-Uzwi**: Cellular assimilation in end-organs.

#### C. Pharmacological Darajat (Degrees of Drug Potency)
- **1st Degree (*Darja Awwal*)**: Mild nutritive/tonic; action imperceptible in health, therapeutic in mild illness.
- **2nd Degree (*Darja Dom*)**: Distinct physiological action; safe across standard dosage ranges.
- **3rd Degree (*Darja Som*)**: Potent perturbation; narrow therapeutic window; requires monitoring.
- **4th Degree (*Darja Chaharum*)**: Severe physiological disturbance or toxic; strictly requires *Muslih* (corrective agent) and specialized *Tadbeer* (processing).
