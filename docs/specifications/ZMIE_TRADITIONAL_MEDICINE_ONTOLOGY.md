# ZMIE TRADITIONAL MEDICINE ONTOLOGY SPECIFICATION
## Ayurveda, TCM, Siddha & Sowa-Rigpa Canonical Epistemic Architectures

---

### 1. Ayurveda Epistemic Architecture (Section 6)

Ayurveda operates on the foundational paradigm of the *Pancha Mahabhutas* (Five Great Elements) condensing into biological forces:

#### A. Tridosha (Biological Regulators)
- **Vata (Akasha + Vayu)**: Kinetic principle governing motion, respiration, nervous impulses.
- **Pitta (Tejas + Jala)**: Thermal principle governing metabolism, thermogenesis, digestion.
- **Kapha (Jala + Prithvi)**: Structural principle governing cohesion, lubrication, tissue building.

#### B. Agni & Ama (Metabolic Fire & Endotoxins)
- **Jatharagni**: Central digestive fire residing in Amashaya and Grahani.
- **Mandagni**: Sluggish hypoactive fire leading to the incomplete breakdown of food, generating **Ama** (unassimilated toxic macromolecular intermediate).

#### C. Dravya Pharmacology (Rasa Panchaka)
Every medicinal substance is characterized by five intrinsic properties:
1. **Rasa (Taste)**: Madhura, Amla, Lavana, Tikta, Katu, Kashaya.
2. **Guna (Qualities)**: Guru (heavy), Laghu (light), Snigdha (unctuous), Ruksha (dry), etc.
3. **Virya (Potency)**: Ushna (hot) or Shita (cold).
4. **Vipaka (Post-Digestive Effect)**: Madhura, Amla, or Katu.
5. **Prabhava (Specific Action)**: Unique non-rationalized pharmacological effect.

---

### 2. Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) Architecture (Section 7)

TCM organizes physiology around functional organ networks (*Zang-Fu*) and energetic substances:
- **Zang Organs (Yin)**: Heart (*Xin*), Liver (*Gan*), Spleen (*Pi*), Lung (*Fei*), Kidney (*Shen*).
- **Fu Organs (Yang)**: Small Intestine, Large Intestine, Stomach (*Wei*), Gallbladder, Bladder, San Jiao.
- **Fundamental Substances**: Qi, Blood (*Xue*), Essence (*Jing*), Spirit (*Shen*), Body Fluids (*Jin-Ye*).

---

### 3. Siddha & Sowa-Rigpa Architecture

- **Siddha (Tamil Tradition)**: Mukkutram (Vali, Azhal, Iyyam); 96 Tattvas; specialized mineral and metallic alchemy (*Rasa Chenduram*).
- **Sowa-Rigpa (Classical Tibetan)**: Three humors (Lung, Tripa, Beken); *Gyushi* (*Four Tantras*); pulse and urine diagnosis.
